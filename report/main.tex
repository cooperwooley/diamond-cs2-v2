\section*{Acknowledgements and attribution}
\label{sec:ack}
The denoiser and upsampler build on the DIAMOND code base of Alonso et~al.~\cite{alonso2024diamond}
(MIT license) and on our v1 adaptation of it to Counter-Strike footage. The 51-dimensional action space and the
mouse-bin values are those of Pearce and Zhu~\cite{pearce2021counterstrike}. Frames are rendered with
HLAE~\cite{hlae}, and player state is read from demo files with demoparser2~\cite{demoparser2}. Licenses and
source commits are listed in the repository's \texttt{ATTRIBUTION.md}.
